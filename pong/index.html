Pong

Controls
  • Left paddle: W / S (in 1 player mode the arrow keys work too)
  • Right paddle: ↑ / ↓
  • Touch or mouse: drag on your half of the field
  • Gamepad: left stick or d-pad, Start to pause
  • P or Esc to pause, M to toggle sound

First to 11 wins.
