Connect Four

Controls
  • Click or tap a column to drop a disc there
  • ← / → to choose a column, Enter, Space or ↓ to drop
  • 1–7 drop straight into that column
  • Gamepad: d-pad or stick left / right, A to drop, Start for this menu
  • Esc for this menu, M to toggle sound

Get four of your discs in a row: across, up or diagonally.
