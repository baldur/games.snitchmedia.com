Controls
  • Paddle: ← / → or A / D
  • Touch or mouse: drag or move over the field
  • Gamepad: left stick or d-pad, A to launch, Start to pause
  • Space or tap to launch the ball
  • P or Esc to pause, M to toggle sound

Where the ball meets the paddle sets its angle. Outlined bricks take more than one hit.
