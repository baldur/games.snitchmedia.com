Kayak

A first-person marathon kayak race. Find a rhythm, keep your balance, and sit on someone's wash to save your strength for the sprint.

Your boat

Turn your phone sideways to race

Controls
  • ← → paddle strokes. Alternate them on a steady beat, about 95 a minute when racing: rhythm is speed. Uneven strokes, two on one side, steering hard and bumping into people all knock you off it
  • The start: you begin behind the field. Paddle up into a gap on the line, stop, and wait for the horn. It comes without warning after "Are you ready?" Go early and everyone waits while the line comes up to you
  • A D lean to keep your balance. Standing still is wobbly; speed steadies you
  • Q E rudder. It only bites when you're moving
  • Tuck in right behind a boat, or alongside its back half, to ride its wash: the same effort goes further, but the boat rocks more
  • Race distances and times are shown four times longer than you actually paddle, so a 6 km race takes about 7 minutes
  • Gamepad: LT RT strokes, left stick rudder, right stick lean
  • Phone, held sideways: tap the left or right half of the screen to stroke, and slide a finger that's down left or right to steer. Turn the phone like a steering wheel to keep the horizon level: that's your balance. (Tilt needs the page over HTTPS on iPhone; without it, balance is done for you)
  • P or Esc to pause, M to toggle sound
