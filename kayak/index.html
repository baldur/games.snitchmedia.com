Kayak

A first-person marathon kayak. This is the practice river: a 1 km time trial while the racing gets built.

Controls
  • ← → paddle strokes. Alternate them, and find a rhythm: hammering too fast catches less water, and two on one side is a weak steering stroke
  • A D lean to keep your balance. Standing still is wobbly; speed steadies you
  • Q E rudder. It only bites when you're moving
  • Gamepad: LT RT strokes, left stick rudder, right stick lean
  • Touch: tap the left or right half of the screen to stroke
  • P or Esc to pause, M to toggle sound
